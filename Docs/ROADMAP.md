# Roadmap

## Prototype — current

- RealityKit 3D farm, lighting, and procedural art
- Touch orbit/zoom camera and contextual gameplay HUD
- Animated crop growth, windmill, clouds, and harvest feedback
- Accessible field selection and Reduce Motion support
- Local farm plots
- Three crops with distinct timers
- Harvest inventory and market sales
- Local persistence
- Remote repository seam

## Next

- Authored 3D models and richer crop growth stages
- Character movement, interaction animation, and sound design
- Physical-device rendering/performance profiling
- Upgrade catalog (more plots, shorter growth, larger harvest)
- Seed inventory and shop
- Sound, haptics, and onboarding
- Localization and broader accessibility testing

## Online milestone

- Account identity and local-save migration
- Standalone API service
- Server-authoritative economy and time
- Offline action queue and conflict handling
- Friends, visits, and shared events
